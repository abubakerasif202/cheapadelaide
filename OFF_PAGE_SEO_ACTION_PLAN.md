## 4. Customer Review Acquisition & Review Velocity Framework

### The Safe Review Acquisition Workflow
Google actively detects artificial review spikes and IP clustering (e.g. multiple reviews submitted from the same WiFi network or tablet at a company depot).

```
Move Completed Successfully
       │
       ▼
Wait 2 to 4 Hours (Customer settled into new home)
       │
       ▼
Automated Friendly SMS / WhatsApp Message
       │
       ▼
Direct Google Review Shortlink (e.g. https://g.page/r/.../review)
       │
       ▼
Customer Leaves Rating & Contextual Review
       │
       ▼
Owner Response Within 24 Hours (Mentioning suburb and service type)
```

### Review Request Template (SMS)
> *"Hi [Customer Name], thank you for choosing Cheap Adelaide Removalist for your move to [Destination Suburb] today! If the team did a great job taking care of your furniture, could you take 30 seconds to leave us a quick Google review? It helps our local Adelaide team immensely: [GBP_SHORT_URL] - Thank you!"*

### Owner Response Strategy (AEO & Local SEO Booster)
Always respond to every review within 24 hours. Naturally include the suburb and moving service in your reply:
> *"Thank you for the kind words, [Customer Name]! We were delighted to help with your 3-bedroom house move in [Mawson Lakes]. Wishing you all the best in your new home!"*

### Review Velocity Guidelines
- **Target Velocity:** 2 to 4 genuine reviews per week.
- **Natural Growth:** Steady weekly accumulation signals authentic business volume.
- **Never Incentivize:** Do not offer discounts, gift cards, or cash for reviews (violates Google Terms of Service and ACCC guidelines).
